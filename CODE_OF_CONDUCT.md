# Code of Conduct

GhostReplay Lab adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of conduct.
The full text is published at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ and applies to this
project as if it were reproduced here.

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful to everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of
  experience.
- Discuss ideas, code and designs, not people. Give and accept constructive feedback
  graciously.
- Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and
  publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

This summary is for convenience only; the Contributor Covenant 2.1 text linked above is the
authoritative version.

## Scope

The code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews and any other channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents
the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **`<CONTACT_EMAIL>`**. All reports are
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers will respect the privacy and
safety of the person who reports an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct in good faith may face
consequences determined by the other members of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction,
warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), choosing the response that fits the impact of the
behaviour.
